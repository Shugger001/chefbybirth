Frequently Asked

How far in advance should I order?
Same-day orders are welcome for standard quantities. For 10+ kenkey pieces, please order at least 24 hours ahead so we can ferment and prepare properly.
Do you deliver?
Yes! We deliver within select Pennsylvania ZIP codes. Delivery is $5, or free on orders over $40. Enter your full address with ZIP at checkout.
Is kenkey gluten-free?
Kenkey is made from fermented corn and is naturally gluten-free. Shito may contain shellfish — let us know about allergies in special instructions.
How do I pay?
We confirm your order via WhatsApp and arrange payment at pickup or upon delivery — cash, Venmo, or Zelle.
Can I customize spice level?
Absolutely! Choose mild, medium, hot, or extra hot at checkout. Our shito ranges from flavorful to seriously spicy.
